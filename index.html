Rooted in Beirut

A Cornerstone of Medical Care Facing LAU Lower Gate Since 1976

Established in the heart of Ras Beirut, our pharmacy began as a modest neighborhood apothecary dedicated to students, faculty, and families residing along Hamra, Manara, and Koraytem.

Through decades of urban evolution, Ras Beirut Pharmacy remained an unwavering beacon of health support. Today, we bridge this rich heritage with modern digital telehealth, offering automated refills, interactive health tools, and instant delivery.
